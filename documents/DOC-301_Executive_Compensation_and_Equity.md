## Executive Salaries for Fiscal Year 2026
- Chief Executive Officer (CEO): Base salary of ₹4.50 crore per annum with a 35% target performance bonus linked to ARR milestones.
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO): Base salary of ₹3.20 crore per annum with a 25% annual performance bonus.
- Chief Technology Officer (CTO): Base salary of ₹3.50 crore per annum with a 30% retention bonus.

## Equity Incentives & Pool
The executive long-term equity incentive pool comprises 5.0% of diluted common shares, vesting over a 4-year period with a 1-year cliff.
